# Design

Use the native 1440 by 860 simulator canvas. The aircraft and solid wall
openings remain the focal content. Flight readings and the selected action
are on the right; the actual trajectory map is below. The rasterized Segoe UI
and Consolas text is already part of the footage, so no DOM typography is
needed.

Keep the navy background, alternating wall colors and orange trajectory.
Use one uninterrupted video at playback rate 1. No additional camera motion,
music, narration, cropping or artificial transitions. Hold the final view
for two seconds with its four-of-eight gate count and exhausted-credit label.
