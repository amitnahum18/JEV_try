# Design

Preserve the 1440 by 860 simulator canvas. The aircraft and open green frames
are the focal content, with flight data on the right and the actual trajectory
map below. Passed frames turn blue. No wall panels, crop, music, narration,
new text overlay or artificial camera motion.

Use the native navy background and existing rasterized Segoe UI and Consolas
HUD fonts. Play one continuous footage asset at 1x and hold its real completion
screen for two seconds.
