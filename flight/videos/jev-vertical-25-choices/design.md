# Design

Use the actual 1440 by 860 simulator canvas. The airplane and open green
frames are the focal content; flight readings are on the right and the
measured trajectory is below. Passed frames turn blue. Preserve the native
navy background, colors and rasterized Segoe UI/Consolas text.

One continuous clip at 1x, with a two-second hold on its true terminal
state. The video must show two passed frames and the missed third frame.
Do not add a success card or change the recorded aircraft trajectory.
